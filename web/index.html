SeerStone: Joseph's Journey

A 2D Adventure of Faith, Revelation & Perseverance (1820–1844)

Prototype v0.2 — Playable Now!

Experience the First Vision in the Sacred Grove.

Desktop recommended • Mobile touch supported

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys — Move
  • E or Tap — Interact near glowing objects
  • Tab — Open Journal / Faith
  • Click — Choose dialogue options

📱 On mobile: Touch and drag to move • Tap to interact

The Story

Step into 1820 New York as young Joseph Smith. Explore the Sacred Grove, witness the First Vision, and begin one of history’s most remarkable journeys.

This is an educational prototype built in Godot 4. More locations, seer stone puzzles, and full narrative coming soon.
